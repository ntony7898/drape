Privacy Policy

Drape (working title) (test build) · Effective October 26, 2026 · Developer: Tony Nguyen (GitHub: ntony7898)

Draft stub for the test build — to be finalised before an App Store release or before a backend is connected.

What the test build stores

  • Your saved designs (recipe, painted texture, thumbnails, uploaded artwork), daily-challenge entries and streak are stored in your browser (IndexedDB / local storage) on this device only. “Reset on-device data” in Settings removes them.
  • Rendering, cloth simulation, photo and video exports happen on your device. Nothing is uploaded.

Sharing

Share links contain the design recipe (garment, fabric, colors, pattern, fit, text/shape decals). Uploaded images and painting are never included. Files you share via your device share sheet are governed by the receiving app.

Demo leaderboard

Designers marked DEMO are fictional and generated on your device.

No tracking

No analytics, ads, cookies or third-party requests. Fonts are bundled with the app.

Future versions

If accounts and a server are added, this policy will be updated to describe what is collected (e.g. account email, posts, reports), why, how long it is kept, and how to delete your account in-app.
